Family Beginnings

All of this started from a family bonding experience when we acquired a large lot of cards from a family friend. Upon attaining the cards, we spent countless time doing research on card sets, value, and market rates. Through this extensive time together, my stepfather Sam, my younger brother Zachary, and I (Jaden) were able to find a common ground in our new favorite hobby; Card Collecting

The Road Ahead

Beginning in March with our very first show, we've been able to learn very valuable information and retain a steady business. We experienced proper sales, big and small, and met many bother vendors and collectors everyday. These new people teach us various things about the hobby we all love, which we use to continue our path. We have grown as partners, the three of us, and strive to continue building this up day by day. There is still much to be learned about being a Card Vendor, but nothing that doesn't come with time.
