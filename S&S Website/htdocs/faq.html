Feel free to look through some of our frequently asked questions we have heard recently. Can't find the answer you are looking for? Feel free to message us directly from our information in the Contact tab or in the message board below.

Where is our company based and where should I expect my orders to be shipped from?
Currently, Sam and Sons: Cards and Collectables© is based in the northern region of Indiana, stemming from Elkhart County, Indiana. Any and all orders will be sent out directly from any neighboring post offices in the area.
How do we determine all of our prices for our items?
As of right now, with lack of subscription services, pricing our items has been a strenuous task. However, we had much luck using past sales on Ebay and current value checking websites, such as sportscardpro and 130pt. That being said, all prices were instated the day the offer went live. In the card collecting hobby, it is common to see card values fluctuate as time passes. Feel free to shoot us an offer based on recent comps. All prices are negotiable.
What exactly are you interested in purchasing?
We specialize in many different areas, spanning various sports. Personally, we especially enjoy collecting and purchasing more vintage cards, specifically baseball. That being said, we also expand further into vintage basketball and more modern sets of the big three (Football, Baseball, Basketball). We are interested in anything really, but we prefer buying bigger lots at a discounted price to further our collections and to restock our table. Don't be afraid to send a inquiry describing singles, we'd love to see what sort of deal we can make.
How do we go about shipping?
Depending on the size of the order, we typically ship cards in sleeves and toploaders, sealed inside a bubble mailer to keep the cards safe at all cost.
Are we open to trades?
Yes! We are willing to work with any customer to ensure that they are satisfied with the trade, whether its monetary value or sentimental value.
How important is the condition of the card to your company?
The condtion of any cards we receive from orders or trades in failry relevant. Its condition allows us to give competitive prices and the conditon of the card can and will affect its value and place in the current market. If we believe we cannot move it, the chances of making a deal drop.
How often is your inventory updated?
Our inventory changes frequently based on sales, new purchases, and card show acquisitions. We recommend checking the Services page regularly or visting our company socials. This is where you could find current sales and invetory changes.
Can you help new collectors get started?
Yes. We enjoy helping beginners learn the hobby, understand card values, and build their first collection. Feel free to reach out with any questions about getting started.
Are you willing to help struggling vendors sell their inventory?
Of course! If you are struggling to move your cards, get into contact with us and we will come to an agreement on a consignment deal.

Have a question?

Don't hesitate to ask us anything. We are here to support you on your card collecting journey!
